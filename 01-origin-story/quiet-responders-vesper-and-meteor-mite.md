# Two quiet responders: Vesper and Meteor Mite

Week-by-week walkthrough of two of the four responders who went quiet after
release 4.2 (Wed 12 Aug 2026). Source: `pings` and `callouts` tables in the
Rook database (data to 6 Sep), plus the 3 Sep interview with Aunt Dot and the
4 Sep interview with Kip. Weeks start on Mondays.

Evidence is from the data and interviews. Anything about *why* is inferred
from code plus data and is unconfirmed with engineering (scores aren't stored
and answer times aren't recorded).

## Vesper (handler: Aunt Dot)

**Before 4.2 (29 Jun to 3 Aug, six weeks):** 13 to 15 pings a week. Took 10 to
13 each week, turned down 1 to 3, missed 0 or 1. Said yes to almost everything
in Old Town (70 of 72) and took nothing outside it (0 of 14).

| Week starting | Pings | Taken | Turned down | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 Jun | 14 | 11 | 3 | 0 |
| 6 Jul | 13 | 10 | 2 | 1 |
| 13 Jul | 15 | 13 | 1 | 1 |
| 20 Jul | 14 | 11 | 3 | 0 |
| 27 Jul | 13 | 11 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 Aug | 14 | 12 | 2 | 0 |
| **10 Aug (4.2 ships Wed)** | 12 | 6 | 2 | **4** |
| 17 Aug | 5 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| 24 Aug | 2 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 31 Aug | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

- **Week of 10 Aug:** the first misses came on 13 Aug, the day after release.
  All four were in Hillcrest or Lakeshore, outside Old Town. Two Hillcrest pings about
  three hours apart on 16 Aug were both missed. Still took four Old Town pings.
- **Week of 17 Aug:** 5 pings. The last pickup in the file was Mon 17 Aug.
  The 19 Aug miss was in Old Town, the one area Vesper used to answer reliably.
- **Week of 24 Aug:** 2 pings, nothing taken.
- **Week of 31 Aug:** one ping (1 Sep), missed. No pings after that to 6 Sep.
- **After 4.2 in total:** 17 pings, 5 taken (all in the first six days), 3
  turned down, 9 missed.
- **Old Town after 4.2:** 4 of 10 pings missed, against 0 of 72 before.
- **Ping mix:** 16% of pings were outside Old Town before 4.2 (14 of 86), 41%
  after (7 of 17). Vesper took none of those either time. Why the mix shifted
  is not known.
- **Tickets:** none about Vesper.

**Aunt Dot (3 Sep, her words):** a ping buzzes on the counter, Vesper comes
downstairs two at a time, and by the time a thumb is on the screen "it's
gone". There were also quiet stretches when she cooked for someone "home all
week instead of half of it". She hadn't connected the two until asked. She'd
like to be told too when something comes in.

## Meteor Mite (handler: Kip, who also looks after The Gale)

**Before 4.2 (29 Jun to 3 Aug, six weeks):** 10 to 12 pings a week. Took 6 to
9 each week, turned down 2 to 4, missed 0 or 1. Took pings in Eastgate (48 of
62, with 12 turned down and 2 missed). Outside Eastgate (Midtown, Foundry Row)
all 8 pings were turned down.

| Week starting | Pings | Taken | Turned down | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 Jun | 11 | 7 | 4 | 0 |
| 6 Jul | 12 | 9 | 3 | 0 |
| 13 Jul | 10 | 6 | 4 | 0 |
| 20 Jul | 11 | 7 | 3 | 1 |
| 27 Jul | 12 | 9 | 2 | 1 |
| 3 Aug | 11 | 8 | 3 | 0 |
| **10 Aug (4.2 ships Wed)** | 10 | 4 | 2 | **4** |
| 17 Aug | 4 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 24 Aug | 2 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 31 Aug | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

- **Week of 10 Aug:** two takes on Mon and Tue, before release. From 13 Aug,
  three misses in about 18 hours (Midtown 13:43, Eastgate 15:12, Eastgate
  07:26 the next morning). Not unresponsive: two Eastgate pings were taken
  within about four hours on 15 Aug, then another miss on 16 Aug.
- **Week of 17 Aug:** 4 pings. The last pickup was Wed 19 Aug.
- **Week of 24 Aug:** 2 pings: one missed (Sat 22 Aug), one turned down
  (27 Aug).
- **Week of 31 Aug:** one Eastgate ping on 29 Aug, missed. Nothing from 30 Aug
  to 4 Sep, then a Midtown ping on 5 Sep, missed.
- **After 4.2 in total:** 14 pings, 3 taken, 3 turned down, 8 missed.
- **Eastgate after 4.2:** 5 of 9 pings missed, against 2 of 62 before.
- **Ping mix:** 11% of pings were outside Eastgate before (8 of 70), 36% after
  (5 of 14). None taken either time.
- **Tickets:** none about Meteor Mite, and none about The Gale.

**Kip (4 Sep, his words):** Meteor Mite "has been texting me all week asking if
something's broken because it's so quiet", and thinks they've been forgotten.
The Gale "hasn't stopped" and is exhausted. His reply to Meteor Mite was "hang
in there, it'll pick up". He couldn't explain it: "I just watch the cards."
(The Gale went from about 13 pings a week to 21 in the same period.)

## What this does and doesn't show

- **Shows:** both responders missed a burst of pings right after 4.2, then
  their pings fell to about one a week and have not recovered. Neither filed a
  ticket or was named in one. Both missed pings in their home area, which they
  almost never missed before.
- **Inferred, not confirmed:** misses lowering a responder's ranking
  (`history.py` scores a miss the same as a turn-down; scores aren't stored).
- **Unknown:** why these two missed so much more than the other twelve (59% of
  pings missed for the four, against 14% for the rest). Answer times, phone
  delivery and availability aren't in the data.
